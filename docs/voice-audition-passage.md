# Ene English audition passage

Prepared for TASK-023. No operator recording has been captured yet. The current listening room uses a short public-domain reference; this passage is for the later comparison using your own voice.

Read the passage once in a comfortable everyday voice, then again with a cheerful, expressive delivery. Keep the microphone position and input gain the same. Do not strain to change your pitch. Aim for roughly 60–90 seconds including the short pauses and reactions; keep normal and expressive takes as separate local files.

> Hey everyone! I'm so glad you're here. Let's try something fun today!
>
> I’ve been working on a little project, and I think it’s finally ready. There’s a blue light on the desk, a warm drink beside me, and about twelve things I still want to test. But first, I want to know how your day is going.
>
> Wait, really? That actually worked! Okay, one more try.
>
> Three tiny robots brought fresh strawberries, blue ribbons, and twelve little bells. The smallest robot said, “Please put the purple ribbon next to the yellow one.” That sounds simple, but somehow we ended up with a very confused robot and a beautifully decorated chair.
>
> I think we should take the left path. What do you think?
>
> Let’s slow down for a second. I can explain what happened, and then we can try it together. There’s no rush. We’ll figure it out.
>
> Oh! I didn’t expect that! That’s wonderful. Thank you for being here with me.

After each take, add a quiet “Can you hear this clearly?”, a comfortable laugh and about ten seconds of spontaneous speech about something you enjoyed recently. Do not force a laugh or share private details for the test.

Use identical recordings for every candidate, retain the original WAV files locally and create level-matched comparison copies. During listening, rate intelligibility, naturalness, character fit, effort/comfort and difficult sounds. Speaking effort and live conversation cannot be scored from the existing reference speaker. The final two candidates also need a real conversation at the selected live buffer settings.
