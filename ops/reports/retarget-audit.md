# Retarget solver audit and reusable math helpers

Reviewed 2026-09-12 for TASK-010 through TASK-013. The findings below preserve the initial audit; the integration checkpoint at the end records the completed corrections. This report does not certify live webcam tracking or close those tasks.

## Priority findings and minimal fixes

| Priority | Finding in the reviewed solver | Recommended correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | `aim()` converts world goals through the rendered parent's previous orientation while all new ancestor goals remain unapplied. Moving an upper arm therefore changes the reference used by its forearm/hand on a later iteration. | Store world goals. Cache current world quaternions before modifying any bones. Apply in hierarchy order: smooth each cached world quaternion toward its world goal, then convert with the updated parent's inverse. Preserve the avatar rest rotation. |
| P0 | Head camera orientation is assigned directly to the head's local quaternion while the spine also receives the torso rotation. Shared torso/head turns can be counted twice. The calibration multiplication `neutral^-1 * current` expresses its delta in neutral-local axes. | Extract a valid rotation; compute world delta `current * neutral^-1`, clamp that delta, and multiply the head rest-world orientation. Convert through the updated parent as above. Define neck/head ownership explicitly; if motion is distributed, let head compensate for the already-applied neck world rotation. |
| P0 | The face matrix is passed directly to `Quaternion.setFromRotationMatrix`, although MediaPipe's format permits uniform scale. The update path does not reject nonfinite or degenerate matrices, and normalization alone cannot remove angle distortion caused by scale. | Validate a finite positive uniform-scale affine basis, reject reflections/shear/degeneracy, then use `Matrix4.extractRotation`. The new `faceRotation()` does this. Sanitize expression coefficients independently so a bad orientation matrix need not suppress otherwise valid facial coefficients. |
| P0 | Hand identity comes only from the handedness category, and later same-category detections can overwrite earlier ones. The category score is treated as general hand quality. | Associate the hand's image wrist with pose landmarks 15/16 using one-to-one matching, distance and ambiguity gates. Keep ambiguous hands unowned. Use a high-confidence unique label only for an unavailable pose wrist. Classification uncertainty alone need not discard a strong pose-wrist match. |
| P1 | Wrist aiming uses only wrist→middle MCP. That direction cannot identify rotation about the palm's long axis. | Form a palm basis from wrist, middle, index and little MCP positions. Apply observed-basis × inverse(rest-basis) × rest-world orientation. Reject degenerate geometry and conservatively gate an observed edge-on palm. Blend to a pose-derived wrist orientation when the hand observation disappears. |
| P1 | Finger segments are independently aimed in world space with no bend limits. Distal rest direction falls back to `node.position`, which is parent-local even though the aiming function interprets it as world-space. | Use parent-relative signed flexion and modest MCP spread limits, with a separate thumb opposition/flexion rule. Clamp meaningful anatomical ranges and preserve a bend-plane sign. For terminal joints, derive a rest tip direction from a real end joint or a world-space continuation of the preceding phalanx. Do not mix local offsets and world directions. |
| P1 | Elbows/knees have no bend-plane continuity or hinge limits; standing never rotates the hips, and foot aiming/root offsets do not implement floor contact. | Use shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle triples to determine a bend plane. Near straight limbs, transport the previous valid plane or use a side-specific torso-relative fallback; do not infer a plane from a near-zero cross product. Add hinge limits and angular-speed limits. Solve hip orientation separately from spine lean. Keep bounded root translation and explicitly document any foot-contact behavior not implemented. |
| P1 | The outer frame timestamp controls all freshness, although worker pose and hands samples are cached between inference calls. `now - timestamp < 500` also accepts future timestamps. | Gate face, pose and hands with their own `samples.*.timestamp` and `present` flag, including nonnegative finite ages. Track losses per limb and fade to idle without retaining a last extreme goal. Add landmark presence/visibility and in-image checks where appropriate. |

For parent-first application, a desired world quaternion `W` should be smoothed from the bone's cached pre-update world quaternion, then assigned as `inverse(updatedParentWorld) * smoothedW`. Slerping old local quaternions after their parents move still compounds ancestor motion during the transition. Idle local poses can intentionally follow their updated parent; keep this fallback behavior distinct from an observed world-orientation goal.

For the current frontal tracking basis, torso world goal `torsoDelta * spineRestWorld` and head goal `headDelta * headRestWorld` preserve rest orientation. If a different scene/camera alignment is introduced later, conjugate measurement deltas through that alignment explicitly. Preview mirroring belongs to presentation and must not swap solver bone ownership.

## Verified coordinate and handedness sources

The current face-geometry implementation converts top-left image Y into an upward metric axis, then flips Z when crossing into its right-handed metric coordinates. Its transformation quaternion already uses that metric frame; the landmark adapter `(x, -y, -z)` must not be applied again to it. This conclusion follows directly from [`geometry_pipeline.cc`, ProjectXY and ChangeHandedness](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/tasks/cc/vision/face_geometry/libs/geometry_pipeline.cc#L196-L235). The [face geometry transform definition](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/tasks/cc/vision/face_geometry/proto/face_geometry.proto#L44-L55) allows translation, rotation and uniform scale. Three.js requires an unscaled rotational basis for [`setFromRotationMatrix`](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Quaternion.html#setFromRotationMatrix).

The current Tasks hand graph maps class 0 to `Right` and class 1 to `Left`; see [`ConfigureTensorsToHandednessCalculator`](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/tasks/cc/vision/hand_landmarker/hand_landmarks_detector_graph.cc#L116-L129). The legacy implementation instead uses the opposite ordering in its [`handedness.txt`](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/modules/hand_landmark/handedness.txt), and its [legacy Hands documentation](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/docs/solutions/hands.md#multi_handedness) explicitly assumes mirrored input. The current [Tasks web guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker/web_js#handle_and_display_results) does not state that legacy mirroring assumption. Therefore the legacy instruction is insufficient evidence for adding a blanket label swap to Tasks. The new association helper keeps current labels unchanged as a weak fallback. A labelled-image inference with the exact installed binary and a physical left/right camera check remain separate validation work.

Hand world coordinates are measured around each hand's geometric center, while pose world coordinates are measured around the hip midpoint. Use the shared normalized image coordinates for wrist association; independent world-coordinate origins cannot be compared as positions. Relative hand-segment vectors remain useful because their common hand origin cancels. These origins are documented in the [Hand Landmarker web guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker/web_js#handle_and_display_results) and [Pose Landmarker web guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker/web_js#handle_and_display_results). The Tasks graph exposes handedness classification separately from presence confidence, so the handedness category score is not a general visibility score; see [the graph's output separation](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/tasks/cc/vision/hand_landmarker/hand_landmarks_detector_graph.cc#L288-L306).

## Implemented helpers and verification

Only [src/retarget-math.ts](../../src/retarget-math.ts), [tests/retarget-math.test.ts](../../tests/retarget-math.test.ts) and this report were authored for this implementation subtask. Core solver and controller integration belongs to the parent task.

| Helper | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `faceRotation(matrix)` | Returns a normalized quaternion or null for malformed, nonfinite, degenerate, reflected or sheared input. Positive uniform face scale is removed. |
| `calibratedHeadWorld(current, neutral, restWorld, limits?)` | Returns a clamped camera/world-space calibrated goal, with limits applied before the avatar rest rotation. |
| `palmWorldRotation(restPoints, observedPoints, restWorld, options?)` | Uses wrist/middle/index/little render-space positions and both palm axes. Default observed `minFacingCos` is 0.06; zero permits a fully edge-on observation. Rest geometry is not camera-facing gated. |
| `associateHands(hands, poseImage, options?)` | Returns `{left?, right?}` observations. Pass actual capture `imageAspect` when available. Distances are in image-height units; defaults are maximum distance 0.18, ambiguity margin 0.035 and missing-wrist label threshold 0.85. Prior wrist positions are an optional weak continuity hint. |

Validation commands:

```powershell
npx.cmd vitest run tests/retarget-math.test.ts
npx.cmd tsc --noEmit
```

Result: 15 focused tests passed and TypeScript exited 0. Tests cover signed face axes at several scales, invalid transforms, noncommuting calibration rotations, rest orientation and clamping, palm pronation with an unchanged wrist-to-middle direction, edge-on/degenerate palms, crossing hands, one-to-one ownership, ambiguous overlaps, distance rejection, weak-label fallback, capture aspect and malformed observations.

Required integration fixtures still include a torso/head turn counted exactly once, parent/child world-goal accuracy during rapid ancestor changes, all-chain nonfinite failure injection, signed finger curl on Ene, elbow/knee near-straight plane continuity, separate sample staleness, and occlusion/reacquisition with bounded angular speed. Real head/blink/arm/hand/knee acceptance remains unperformed by this audit.

## Integration checkpoint

The parent implementation integrated these helpers in `src/retarget.ts`, added `src/limb-solver.ts` and `src/finger-solver.ts`, and now applies goals in parent-first world space. Each observed goal carries its own timestamp. All current world quaternions are cached before ancestor changes; head compensation avoids double-counting torso turns. Uniform face scale is removed, face coefficients are sanitized independently, and stale/future observations fade to idle.

Hand association uses actual image aspect and pose wrists, with conservative ambiguity gating. Full palm orientation preserves twist; finger flexion is limited in the parent frame, and distal continuation uses world-space rest directions. Elbow/knee planes stay stable near straight limbs, reject extreme foldbacks and respect fixed segment lengths. Standing hips have separate orientation and bounded root translation. Ground-height correction uses the lowest modeled foot while both observed feet are visible; it is not foot locking or jump capture.

`npm.cmd test` now passes 58 tests across 11 files. The dedicated hierarchy tests verify shared torso/head turns, arm segments under changing ancestors, unchanged bone lengths, independent body-sample expiry, malformed face data, future timestamps and finite recovery. Limb tests cover degenerate/near-straight geometry, bend-plane continuity, angle limits and malformed input; finger tests cover signed limits. These are algorithmic checks, not observed human motion.

`node scripts/retarget_fixture_smoke.mjs` passed eight cases on actual Ene: neutral, shared torso/head turn, blink/mouth, palm twist with conflicting handedness label resolved by wrist position, closed fingers, standing neutral, bent knees with bounded ground correction, and tracking loss. [Structured evidence](retarget-fixture-smoke.json) and images under `local/retarget/` record that run. The palm/fist and standing screenshots were inspected. Physical left/right gestures, crossing hands, occlusion/reacquisition quality and perceived response delay remain open in TASK-010 through TASK-013.
