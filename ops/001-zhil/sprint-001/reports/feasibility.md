# Laptop feasibility checkpoint

Date: 2026-09-12. Decision: diagnose the measured combined capture/animation failure before another baseline acceptance run. Avatar rendering, local tracking initialization/recovery and native Windows capture are proven separately, but the longer combined fixture fell to approximately 1 Hz and produced white capture. The full live camera/voice/OBS workload is not accepted.

Host: Intel Core i7-1255U (10 cores, 12 logical processors), Intel Iris Xe Graphics, driver 31.0.101.4255, Windows 11. Tested tools and exact model notices are in the [toolchain report](toolchain.md).

Evidence: [output](output-smoke.json), [camera recovery](tracking-recovery-smoke.json), [camera/network report](camera-reliability.md), [retarget fixtures](retarget-fixture-smoke.json), [OBS capture](obs-capture.md), and [voice controller](../tasks/active/TASK-P02.md).

The worker maintains one pending bitmap, stamps face/pose/hands separately and reduces pose/hand cadence in Low power. Tests exercise partial GPU initialization cleanup and CPU fallback; the [real CPU diagnostic](tracking-cpu.md) verifies the installed SDK path. It still creates WebGL contexts, so CPU inference is not a guarantee of operation without graphics support. The [positive-photo check](tracking-positive-fixture.md) subsequently exposed and fixed an SDK timestamp failure after face detection: MediaPipe receives elapsed session milliseconds while external freshness timestamps remain unchanged. Its positive landmark timings are materially slower than negative synthetic inputs. Cold GPU positive inference took 18.79 seconds. Render fps and these bounded still-image measurements do not measure live tracking or end-to-end motion latency. [Recording evidence](obs-recording.md) and [server-stop evidence](server-stop.md) remain separate component checks.

The first two combined still-photo attempts used 640×480 input, seated mode, Gentle springs and one clean view with native 720p30 OBS recording. Their strict layout guard exposed a periodic keepalive defect while actual DOM/native capture geometry remained unchanged. The [heartbeat report](output-layout-heartbeat.md) preserves both partial runs, exact browser reproduction and the verified correction. The corrected two-phase measurement completed both 900-second windows but exposed a separate approximately 1 Hz draw/inference-delivery collapse and white native capture; [performance.md](performance.md) records the failed gates, timeline, visual evidence and remaining diagnosis. A nominal 30 fps recording and zero encoder skips did not prove that Ene remained visible or smooth. Physical gestures, permission/device behavior and end-to-end latency still require the operator; the pending camera check has not been reported complete. Exact VMD dance reproduction is outside the baseline, while recognizable standing lean, arm raises, knee bends and small steps remain required.

OBS Virtual Camera registration was canceled at the Windows confirmation prompt; no consumer test is claimed. That does not prevent window capture or local recording tests. The tentative internal vLLM API is unconfigured and does not establish a deployable voice-conversion service.
